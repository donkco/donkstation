Tracking Sticker
/obj/item/sticker/tracking

A sticker with a tiny tracking chip embedded in the adhesive. When stuck to an object, the object becomes trackable by a crypto-chipped crew pinpointer. When peeled off, trackability transfers back to the sticker itself. The sticker itself does not track until its been stuck to something once, so someone could just take it off and drop it in a trash bin if they find it. Can only be seen and removed via double-examine (Handled in our epic element/component/trackable).

Procs

on_peeledWhen peeled off, transfer trackability from host back to self.
on_self_deletedIf the sticker is deleted while stuck to a host, remove the trackable element from that host.
on_stuckWhen stuck to a host, transfer trackability from self to the host.
