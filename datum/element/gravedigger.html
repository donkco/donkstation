gravedigger
/datum/element/gravedigger

Gravedigger element. Allows for graves to be dug from certain tiles. Spawns a turf-appropriate grave subtype and fill material, and adds a soil sample to the tool's forensics.

Vars

turf_grave_dataAssoc list mapping turf type paths to /datum/grave_turf_data subtypes. Listed most-specific first so istype() matching finds the right subtype.
turfs_typecacheLazily-initialized typecache built from the keys of turf_grave_data
