/tg/ Station 13 - Modules - Types — Var Details - Proc Details

vinyl_player
/datum/component/vinyl_player

Component that handles something being able to play vinyl records which lets us play similarly to the jukebox except with records that can be inserted/removed

Vars

base_icon_stateBase icon_state of the parent, cached at init for update_icon_state handling.
comms_playing_volumeVolume when on a comms console (since its direct it can sound a bit louder usually)
item_modeIf TRUE, uses item-mode signals (activate-in-hand) instead of click signals. Comms console linking is not available in item mode.
linked_consoleThe comms console this player is linked to for station-wide broadcast. Currently only supports non-item variants of the vinyl player.
loaded_recordCurrently loaded vinyl record.
music_playerThe jukebox datum driving playback.
playback_offsetPosition of song; saved when paused
playingWhether we are actively playing.
playing_volumeNormal volume
song_timeridTimer ID for detecting when the current song ends.
use_animated_recordWhether we show the record moving with overlays or if we just use an active/off state.

Procs

eject_recordEjects the record, stopping playback and returning it to the user's hands.
link_consoleLinks the vinyl player to a comms console for station-wide broadcast.
load_recordLoads a record into the player and updates the jukebox track.
on_contextHandles contextual screentip context for both item and machinery parents.
on_ejectCalled on RMB (machine) or secondary activate-in-hand (item). Ejects the record.
on_examineAppends record status lines when the parent is examined.
on_item_interactionCalled when an item is used on the parent. Handles record insertion.
on_linked_console_deletedCalled when the linked comms console is deleted. Silently clears the link.
on_multitool_actCalled when a multitool is used on the machinery vinyl player. Toggles comms console link.
on_parent_deletingWhen the parent is deleted, drop any loaded record to the turf so it isn't lost.
on_parent_movedCalled when the machinery vinyl player moves. Disconnects any linked console.
on_play_pauseCalled on LMB (machine) or activate-in-hand (item). Toggles play/pause.
on_update_icon_stateUpdates the parent's icon_state to reflect playback status.
on_update_overlaysDraws the loaded record as an overlay on the parent, with a _spin suffix while playing.
on_wrench_actBlocks wrenching while the record player is actively playing. Unlinks console on unwrench.
pause_playbackPauses playback using SOUND_PAUSED; queries clients for the real playback position.
song_endedCalled by timer when the track finishes naturally.
start_playbackStarts (or resumes) playback from the current offset.
stop_playbackStops playback and resets the offset to zero.
toggle_comms_linkToggles the comms console link. Checks preconditions before linking or unlinking.
toggle_playbackToggles between playing and paused states.
unlink_consoleUnlinks the vinyl player from its comms console.
