/datum/component/remote_control

##remote control

Used on living mobs when they are meant to be remote controlled by another mob Holds a reference to an old mind & body, to put them back in once you stop remote controlling the target mob. When the controlling mob gets knocked down or otherwise incapacitated, the mind will be put back into the old body.
